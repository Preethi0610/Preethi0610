# Hi, I'm Preethi 👋

I'm a **Software Engineer at Citizen Project**, and a recent graduate of **South Dakota State University**, Brookings, SD, with a **Master's in Computer and Information Sciences** focused on **Machine Learning and AI**.

## About Me
My undergrad was in **AI** and **Data Science**, and that's really where my interest in data-driven problem solving started — building ML models and figuring out how AI actually works in real-world systems. Over time I also grew a strong interest in **software engineering**: designing and building applications, and turning ideas into reliable, working systems. I like working at the intersection of both, building practical solutions powered by data and machine learning.

- Currently working as a **Software Engineer** at **Citizen Project**.
- Worked as a **Graduate Research Assistant** on *"Fast and Sustainable Video Anomaly Detection with Continual Learning"*.
- Worked as a **Software Developer** at **South Dakota State University, Enterprise Applications**.
- Experienced with **RAG**, **LLMs**, **VLMs**, and building AI-powered applications end to end.
- Programming in **Python**, **C#**, **Java**, **R**, **JS/TypeScript**, **HTML**, **ASP.NET**.
- Outside of tech, I enjoy **reading books** and exploring new ideas.
